# Phase 1 intent examples

The examples below are evaluation seeds, not claims of measured accuracy. Each utterance must resolve to a deterministic intent and then execute against authenticated Finova data.

## 1. Expense totals
1. How much did I spend this month?
2. What are my expenses this month?
3. Total expense for September?
4. Show my spending this month.
5. इस महीने का कुल खर्च कितना है?
6. इस महीने मैंने कितना खर्च किया?
7. इस महीने का खर्च बताओ।
8. Iss mahine ka total kharcha kitna hai?
9. Mera iss mahine ka kharcha batao.
10. Pichle mahine kitna kharch hua?

## 2. Income totals
1. What is my income this month?
2. How much did I invoice this month?
3. Show total revenue for this month.
4. What are my sales this month?
5. इस महीने की कुल आय कितनी है?
6. इस महीने कितने की billing हुई?
7. मेरी कमाई कितनी है?
8. Iss mahine ki total income kitni hai?
9. Meri iss mahine ki kamai batao.
10. Pichle mahine kitni income hui?

## 3. Outstanding
1. How much is outstanding?
2. What do customers still owe me?
3. Show overdue balances.
4. How much receivable is pending?
5. मेरा कुल बकाया कितना है?
6. ग्राहकों से कितने पैसे लेने बाकी हैं?
7. कौन से invoice overdue हैं?
8. Mera total baaki kitna hai?
9. Customers se kitna paisa lena baaki hai?
10. Kaunse invoices overdue hain?

## 4. Customer outstanding
1. How much does Rahul owe me?
2. What is Rahul's outstanding balance?
3. Show Rahul's unpaid invoices.
4. How much is pending from ABC?
5. राहुल का कितना बकाया है?
6. राहुल से कितने पैसे लेने हैं?
7. ABC का outstanding बताओ।
8. Rahul ka kitna baaki hai?
9. Rahul se kitna paisa lena hai?
10. ABC ka unpaid balance batao.

## 5. Recorded profit
1. What is my profit this month?
2. Show profit for September.
3. Income minus expenses this month?
4. How much did I make after expenses?
5. इस महीने का लाभ कितना है?
6. इस महीने का मुनाफा बताओ।
7. आय में से खर्च घटाने पर कितना बचता है?
8. Iss mahine ka profit kitna hai?
9. Income minus kharcha kitna hai?
10. Mere business ka iss month ka munafa batao.
