# Phase 1 finance glossary

| English | Hindi | Hinglish | Plain meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Expense | खर्च | kharcha | Business money spent |
| Income | आय | income/kamai | Money earned or invoiced |
| Outstanding | बकाया | baaki | Money still to be received |
| Receivable | प्राप्य / मिलने वाले पैसे | milne wale paise | Customer money due to you |
| Invoice | इनवॉइस | invoice | Bill issued to a customer |
| Profit | लाभ / मुनाफा | munafa | Income minus recorded expenses |
| GST | GST | GST | Goods and Services Tax |
| TDS | TDS | TDS | Tax deducted at source |
| Input Tax Credit | इनपुट टैक्स क्रेडिट | input tax credit | Eligible GST credit on business purchases |
| Credit note | क्रेडिट नोट | credit note | Adjustment reducing an invoice amount |
| Payment | भुगतान | payment/bhugtan | Money received or paid |
| Due date | भुगतान की अंतिम तारीख | due date | Date by which payment is expected |
